# Code of conduct

TouchMac follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and help people who are new.
- Discuss the work, not the person. No harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- No sexual language or imagery, and no publishing other people's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

Send reports to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/SuzalShrestha/touchmac/security/advisories/new)
(it is private to the maintainers, and it is not only for security problems) or by email to the address on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer can warn, remove content, or ban
a person from the project, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
